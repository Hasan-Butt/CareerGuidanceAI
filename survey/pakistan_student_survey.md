# PakCareer Survey: Google Form Spec (for Moosa)

**Goal:** collect real responses from Pakistani students so we have local data next to the
145k-row international RIASEC dataset. Sections B and C use **exactly the same questions
and scales** as that dataset, so the two can be merged for training and compared in the
bias check. Section D is the Pakistan-specific part that makes the project original.

**Target:** 25+ responses. Send it to FAST and other college/university WhatsApp groups,
juniors still in FSc/A-Levels, siblings and cousins.
**Deliverable 1 snapshot:** export whatever we have by **22 Oct, 8 PM**. The form can stay open after that.
**Time to fill:** about 8 minutes.

### Form settings
- Do **not** collect email addresses. Do not ask for names or roll numbers.
- Make every question required **except** the ones marked *(optional)*.
- Linear scale questions: use **"Multiple choice grid"** for Sections B and C, so each section is a single grid and it's quick to fill on a phone.
- Link the form to a Google Sheet (Responses → Link to Sheets). Share the sheet with Kabeer and Hasan as **Viewer**.

## Section D: Choosing your degree

Use the same field list for questions 1–3:
**Medicine / Health · Engineering · Computer Science / IT · Business / Commerce · Law ·
Arts & Humanities · Pure Sciences · Other**

1. **Which field would YOU most like to study?** (one choice)
2. **Which field do your PARENTS most want you to study?** (one choice + "They have no preference")
3. **Which field are you actually studying or going to study?** (one choice + "Not decided yet")
4. **Who mainly decided (or will decide) your degree?**: Me / My parents / Both equally / Other relatives / Teachers or counsellors
5. **How much pressure do you feel from family about your degree choice?**: scale 1 (None) to 5 (A lot)
6. **If you are already studying: how satisfied are you with your field?**: scale 1 (Very unsatisfied) to 5 (Very satisfied), plus "Not studying yet"
7. **If you could switch fields with no family objection, would you?**: Yes / Maybe / No / Not studying yet
8. **What would most convince your parents to accept a different field?**: Salary data / Job availability in Pakistan / Success stories of others / Advice from a teacher or counsellor / Nothing would / Other
9. **Father's field of work** *(optional)*: same field list + Government service · Own business · Armed forces · Not working · Prefer not to say
10. **Mother's field of work** *(optional)*: same list + Homemaker
11. **Are you the first in your family to attend university?**: Yes / No

## Attention check (put it in the middle of Section B's grid)

Add one extra row: **"Select 'Neutral' for this row"**. Responses that don't pick 3 get
dropped during cleaning. This is the same idea as the fake words in the RIASEC dataset.
